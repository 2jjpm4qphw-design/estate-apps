How it chooses roads

Each kind of road has a price per mile. A B road is the cheapest; a minor road costs 5% more; lanes, A roads and dual carriageways cost more again; motorways cost twenty times as much, and dirt tracks are never used. Roads through woodland or beside water get cheaper, and roads next to busy traffic dearer. The route is the cheapest total — so it will happily go a good deal further to stay on a B road.

Routing is done by BRouter (brouter.de) on OpenStreetMap data. It knows the road type, surface, speed limit and lane count, but not road names, so turn instructions give directions without naming roads. Timings are this app's own estimate for a Fiat 124 at the pace you choose, not BRouter's.
