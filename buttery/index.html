One question and you're set

Your height, weight, age and waist come from the Still Room — the house already holds them, so there is nothing to type and nothing that can disagree. The only thing it does not know is how much you move.

Connect this device

This phone has not been given the estate credentials. Enter them once and every app on the estate is connected — the Still Room, the Library, all of them.

Height is worked back from 113kg at BMI 32, so correct it if it is wrong — everything else depends on it.

Measure your waist at the belly button, standing, breathing out normally — not where your trousers sit. It is the number that tracks visceral fat best, and the one this app cares most about. If you have not got a tape measure to hand, skip it and add it later.

Movement

10–15 minutes, within half an hour of eating. Blunts the glucose spike far more than the same walk done later.
Resistance work
Twice a week. Protects muscle while you are in a deficit — that matters more at 56 than at 36.
