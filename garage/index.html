Your target pressures

These are yours to set and I have deliberately left them blank. The right figures are on a sticker on the driver’s door pillar — open the door and look at the frame — or in the handbook. They differ by wheel size and by load, so a number I found on the internet would be a guess dressed up as a fact, and a guess is how you end up driving on the wrong pressures for a year.
