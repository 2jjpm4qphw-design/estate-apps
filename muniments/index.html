Add a paper

Hints, never passwords.

You change them regularly, so a stored password would be wrong within a month — and a copy of your Apple password sitting in a homemade app is a bad idea whoever built it. Your passwords belong in the Passwords app, which is end-to-end encrypted and already on this phone. What goes here is the nudge that gets you there.

Add a hint

Write the nudge for you in six months, not for you today. “the usual” is not a hint. Nor is anything that would tell a stranger the answer.

What somebody else might need to find.

This is the one page here worth being findable in a hurry. Consider also putting the same few facts in the iPhone’s own Medical ID, which shows on the lock screen without anyone needing to get into the phone at all.
